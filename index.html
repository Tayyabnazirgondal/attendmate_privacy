Privacy Policy for AttendMate

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Short version: AttendMate is a fully offline app. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AttendMate ("the App", "we", "us") handles user information. AttendMate is developed and published by Daynix Studio. By installing and using the App, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. AttendMate is designed as a fully offline application. Specifically, the App does not collect, transmit, store, or share any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Photos, contacts, files, or any personal media
  • Account credentials (the App has no login or registration system)

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All attendance data you enter in AttendMate — including subject names, target percentages, and attendance counts — is stored locally on your device using standard local database storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not uploaded to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is fully removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data

4. Internet & Network Access

AttendMate does not require an internet connection to function. The App does not make network requests, does not communicate with any server, and works entirely offline.

5. Third-Party Services

AttendMate does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or social media platforms. No third-party SDKs collect data through the App.

6. Advertisements

AttendMate does not display any advertisements.

7. Children's Privacy

AttendMate is safe for users of all ages, including children. Since the App collects no personal information from anyone, it complies with children's privacy regulations by design.

8. Permissions

AttendMate does not request any sensitive Android permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage access. The only permissions used are those automatically granted for basic app functionality on your device.

9. Data Security

Because no data leaves your device, there is no server-side data breach risk. The security of your local attendance records depends on the security of your own device (screen lock, device encryption, etc.).

10. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Use the "Clear All Data" option in the App's Settings screen to delete every subject and all attendance records instantly.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all associated data from your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
